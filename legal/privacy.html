Privacy policy

TomeVault open beta · Last updated August 2026

TomeVault is a tabletop RPG companion app. During open beta we store account and session data in Google Firebase (authentication, Firestore database, file storage) to run the service.

What we collect

Account info (email, display name), session content you create (handouts, chat, characters, notes), and technical logs needed to operate and secure the app.

How we use it

To provide realtime sessions, sync data between players and GMs, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

You can stop using the service anytime. For deletion requests or questions, contact hello@tomevault.app.
